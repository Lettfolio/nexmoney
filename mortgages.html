Our Mortgage Services

Whatever your situation,
we've got you covered

From first-time buyers to seasoned investors, our advisors have the expertise to find the right mortgage for you.

First Time Buyer

Taking your first step onto the property ladder is exciting but can feel daunting. We'll guide you through every step — from finding out how much you can borrow to getting the keys in your hand.

Remortgaging

Coming to the end of your fixed term, or just looking to review your rate? We'll search the market and manage the switch for you. Whether a remortgage reduces your payments will depend on your circumstances and the products available to you at the time.

Home Mover

Moving to a new home means a new mortgage. Whether you're upsizing, downsizing, or moving area, we'll find a deal that works for your next chapter.

Buy-to-Let

Building a property portfolio or renting out a single home? We'll search the market for a buy-to-let mortgage that suits your investment strategy and circumstances.

Not all Buy to Let Mortgages are regulated by The Financial Conduct Authority.

Capital Raising

Use the equity in your home to fund home improvements, debt consolidation, a new car, or other large purchases. We'll explain the options available to you and the costs involved.

Think carefully before securing other debts against your home. The overall cost of repayment of other debts might be more when added to your mortgage. Your home might be repossessed if you do not keep up repayments on your mortgage.

Self Build

Building your dream home from scratch? Self-build mortgages are released in stages as construction progresses. We know exactly which lenders suit this niche — and how to get you approved.

Shared Ownership

Buy a share of a property and pay rent on the remainder. Under the Shared Ownership model introduced in 2021 the minimum initial share can be as low as 10%, while older properties and resales on older leases may still require an initial share of between 25% and 75%. 75% is generally the maximum initial share, and you can usually increase your ownership later through staircasing, often up to 100% (subject to the lease and any restrictions in designated protected areas). We'll help you navigate the scheme step by step.

Right to Buy

If you're a council or housing association tenant, Right to Buy may let you purchase your home at a discount. Eligibility and the level of discount depend on the scheme rules and your individual circumstances.

Rate Review & Product Transfer

Happy with your lender but coming to the end of your deal? We review your existing mortgage and compare a product transfer with your lender against the wider market, so you can see which option suits you best.
How It Works

Getting your mortgage
is simpler than you think

1

Initial Consultation

Tell us about your situation. No obligation, no credit check. We listen first.

2

Market Search

We search hundreds of lenders to find products that suit your circumstances.

3

Application & Underwriting

We prepare and submit your application and liaise with the lender. The lender then underwrites your case, a valuation is arranged and your conveyancer carries out the legal work and searches.

4

Mortgage Offer

Where your application is successful the lender issues its offer, and we support you through to completion. Timescales vary with your circumstances.
